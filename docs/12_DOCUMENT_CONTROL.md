# SwiftOrder V1.1 Document Control

## Current authority

`SwiftOrder V1.1 — Board-Approved Commercial Product & Release Specification`, dated 17 August 2026, is the current product/release authority and supersedes the earlier V1.0 target where they conflict.

## Historical material

The project contains documents describing older states such as v0.7.x/v0.8.4 Beta and older commercial ideas such as setup/support pricing. These should remain available as history, but must be clearly marked historical and must not be treated as the current commercial target.

## Current commercial position

- Local PHP/MySQL installation.
- Monthly local licence.
- Inventory-heavy independent SMB retail/stock-oriented target.
- Basic tax-invoice capability.
- No live payment gateway in V1.
- No SaaS multi-tenancy in V1.

## Update rule

Every significant product/engineering decision should record:

- DECISION
- FACTS
- EVIDENCE
- PROJECT IMPACT
- MARKET IMPACT
- LEGAL IMPACT
- RISK
- RECOMMENDATION
- ACTION
- BLOCKER

Do not silently edit historical documents to make them appear current.
