# SwiftOrder V1.1 Commercial / Legal Evidence Register

**Important:** This is an operational checklist, not legal, tax or regulatory advice.

## Before first paying customer

- Confirm the legal entity that owns/commercialises SwiftOrder.
- Record IP ownership/assignment chain for source, assets and documentation.
- Record third-party licence evidence for Composer packages and other shipped assets.
- Complete appropriate trade-mark/IP checks.
- Approve the monthly local licence model.
- Define support scope, cancellation, update responsibilities, backup responsibilities and customer responsibilities.
- Prepare privacy/data-protection documentation appropriate to the deployment model.
- Define incident/security escalation procedure.
- Obtain South African professional legal and tax review where required.

## Privacy evidence

Record:

- Data collected.
- Purpose for each category.
- Retention/deletion rule.
- Access roles.
- Support access controls.
- Backup handling.
- Disclosure/processor arrangements.
- Data-subject request process.
- Incident response.

## Evidence rule

Do not label the project “POPIA compliant”, “tax compliant” or legally guaranteed merely because a feature exists. Record the exact control, evidence and professional review status.
