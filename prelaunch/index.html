THE SMALL THINGS ADD UP

Orders in one place.
Not five different ones.

A customer message here. A stock note there. A price in a spreadsheet. The business still runs—but keeping the details aligned takes time and attention.

SwiftOrder is being built around the practical middle of the operation: the products, orders, customers and records that need to stay connected for the day to move forward.

03 / A CORE PRINCIPLEYOUR CONNECTION
ISN’T YOUR BUSINESS.

OFFLINE-FIRST, ON PURPOSE

The internet should
be an option—not
the whole foundation.

SwiftOrder’s direction prioritises locally usable core workflows. Connected capabilities can extend the product, without making a permanent connection the assumption behind every task.

↯
Local-first is the product direction.

Specific offline capabilities and sync behaviour will be confirmed as development and testing progress. We won’t pretend those details are finished before they are.
