THE SMALL THINGS ADD UP

Bring the daily work

Orders, product details, sales and stock records all affect each other. When they live in disconnected places, staff have to do the joining-up themselves.

SwiftOrder is designed around the core records a shop or small operator uses to keep trading: products, orders, stock levels and sales activity—with one local installation serving supported devices on the same network.

03 / A CORE PRINCIPLEYOUR CONNECTION
ISN’T YOUR BUSINESS.

HOW SWIFTORDER IS DESIGNED TO RUN

Your business system.
On your network.
Not internet-dependent.

The V1 direction is a locally hosted application and database on one business computer. Other supported devices connect through the same local network and work against that shared installation.

↯
Core trading should continue without internet.

The local host and local network must remain available. Internet-dependent integrations are separate from normal local workflows; installation and recovery experience are still part of the product work.
